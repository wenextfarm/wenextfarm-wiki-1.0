WenextFarm is designed to be safe and user‑friendly for home and classroom use. However, like any electro‑mechanical device, it carries potential risks. Please review the safety information below before assembly or operation — especially when children are present.

> **Safety first** 
> 
> Following these safety guidelines will help prevent accidents and injuries.
{.is-success}


## Hazards

Not all hazards may apply to your specific FarmBot model or configuration, and other risks may exist that are not listed. If you have any questions or concerns about product safety, please [contact us](mailto:into@wenextfarm.com).

| Hazard | Description | Precautions |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Heavy boxes | Shipping boxes may be heavy. | Use proper lifting technique; ask for help if needed. |
| Sharp edges | Aluminum parts may have sharp edges. | Handle with care; wear light gloves during assembly. Sand down sharp edges if desired. |
| Tripping | Power and water lines can be trip hazards. | Route cables safely along the bed or ground; use underground conduits if possible. |
| Pinch points | Moving parts can pinch fingers or catch hair/clothing. | Keep hands, hair, and pets away during operation. Power down before maintenance. |
| Rotary tool debris | Spinning tools may eject soil, dust, or small rocks. | Wear safety glasses; keep a safe distance, especially around children and pets. |
| Electrical shock | Device operates at 24V DC from 120/240V AC supply. | Do not open electronics box or plug/unplug cables while powered. Keep dry. Use GFCI outlet. |
| Flooding | Extended watering may overflow the bed. | Do not leave water on unattended; use timers as a backup. |

## Certifications

The table below shows the components of FarmBot that have been tested and **certified** for safety by a third party.

| Component | Certification | Description |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Power Supply | CE, IP67 | Complies with EU safety standards; dust‑proof and waterproof to 1m depth. |
| Raspberry Pi | RoHS, FCC, CE | See [Raspberry Pi documentation](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/) for full list. |
| Farmduino | CE, RoHS | Complies with EU safety standards and hazardous substance restrictions. |

## Safety features

The following **safety features** have been engineered into the FarmBot to provide a safer user experience and help mitigate various hazards.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| E‑stop button | Physical button on electronics box — press to immediately stop all motion and power. |
| Stall detection | Automatically stops motors if movement errors occur repeatedly, preventing damage or injury. |
| Fuse | Protects electronics from over‑current caused by shorts or malfunctions. |
| Pin guard | Automatically resets peripherals after a timeout to prevent unsafe states (e.g., leaving water on too long). |
