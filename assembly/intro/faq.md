## Is WenextFarm weatherproof/rainproof?

Yes. All electronics are protected by sealed housings, and structural components are made from corrosion‑resistant materials like aluminum, stainless steel, and UV‑stabilized plastics.

![防雨-压缩.jpg](/wiki/防雨-压缩.jpg =700x)

However, WenextFarm is not waterproof — it cannot be submerged. In extremely humid environments, additional precautions may be needed to protect the electronics.

## Can WenextFarm service multiple beds?

Not practically.

Moving the unit between beds requires partial disassembly, reconnecting utilities, and recalibration — too time‑consuming for regular use.

Automated solutions are also impractical: moving the entire system (tracks, toolbay, cables) between beds demands excessive hardware and cost. The far better approach is simply extending your tracks and/or gantry to cover a larger single bed.

Our software does not support multiple beds, and we do not recommend this approach.

> **The best way to cover more area with one WenextFarm**
> 
> Make your tracks longer and/or your gantry wider.

## What is our relationship with FarmBot, and why is FarmBot open‑source?

FarmBot is an open‑source precision agriculture CNC farming project, originally created by Rory Aronson, Rick Carlino, and Tim Evers. It provides a complete open‑source hardware and software platform for automated gardening, enabling anyone to build their own farming robot.

<br>![farmbot官网-首页.jpg](/wiki/farmbot官网-首页.jpg)

WenextFarm is an independent company founded by three passionate FarmBot enthusiasts. We have deeply benefited from everything the FarmBot project has pioneered — from hardware design and software architecture to its open documentation ecosystem. **We sincerely thank the FarmBot team and the entire open‑source community for their extraordinary contributions. Without their openness and sharing, WenextFarm would not exist.**

<br>

We developed our own machine based on the open‑source FarmBot design, with optimizations for cost‑effectiveness, ease of use, and flexibility — all with the mission of making precision farming more accessible and affordable.

> **We are NOT the FarmBot company. If you wish to support the original FarmBot project directly, please visit [Farm.Bot](http://www.farm.bot).**

## Why is an open‑source project also available for sale?
Because FarmBot is open‑source, all source files — including CAD models, software, and documentation — are freely available for anyone to use, modify, and build upon. WenextFarm is built on this open‑source foundation, and we fully embrace the spirit of open collaboration.

![farmbot官网-开源.jpg](/wiki/farmbot官网-开源.jpg)

Open‑source licensing also allows anyone to sell compiled versions — whether as complete hardware kits or hosted software services. This gives you two options:

- **DIY route:** Source everything yourself. It's flexible and cost‑effective, but time‑consuming, and compatibility is not guaranteed.

- **Kit route:** Purchase a ready‑to‑use WenextFarm kit. You save time, get a fully tested and guaranteed product, and receive our dedicated support — so you can focus on using and customizing your machine, rather than struggling with part sourcing and compatibility issues.

Open‑source and commercial sales are not mutually exclusive. Open‑source is a design philosophy that makes technology accessible to everyone; commercial sales are a way to sustain and grow the project. We believe in giving you the freedom to choose the path that best fits your needs — whether that's building from scratch or getting started immediately with a complete kit.

<br>

> **Finally, we would like to thank the FarmBot team and the entire open‑source community once again. WenextFarm would not exist without their openness and generosity. If you'd like to support the original project, we encourage you to visit [Farm.Bot](http://www.farm.bot).**

## Can I extend the tracks or make the gantry wider/taller?
<br>
Yes — and we encourage you to experiment! Our kits are designed for a specific size, but with additional hardware, you can modify them. This requires your own R&D effort and is not guaranteed to work. We currently do not offer custom parts for this, so be ready for some DIY work. Good luck!
