## WenextFarm P1 – Smarter Farming, Lower Cost, 100% FarmBot Compatible


WenextFarm P1 is a precision open-source farming robot built on the proven foundation of FarmBot Genesis. By drawing on the best of FarmBot v1.2 and v1.8, we’ve created a kit that is more affordable, easier to assemble, and fully compatible with the FarmBot ecosystem – so you get all the power of FarmBot at a fraction of the cost.

![wenextfarm-p1.jpg](/wiki/wenextfarm-p1.jpg)

## The Best of Both Worlds
FarmBot Genesis v1.2 set the standard for open-source farming robots – reliable, compact, and backed by a mature ecosystem. FarmBot v1.8 pushed the boundary further with major weight reductions (20% lighter shipping weight), a redesigned gantry beam (27% lighter), a slimmer Z-axis, and a streamlined single-box shipment.

WenextFarm P1 combines the proven reliability of v1.2 with the lightweight, high-performance design of v1.8 – then we optimized the bill of materials, streamlined manufacturing, and passed the savings directly to you.

## Key Advantages
### Cost-Effective Kit 

– We’ve carefully re-engineered the BOM to reduce manufacturing costs without compromising quality or performance. The result: a premium farming robot at a significantly lower price point.

### Fully FarmBot Compatible 

– P1 uses the same V-slot aluminum extrusions, NEMA 17 stepper motors with rotary encoders, Farmduino electronics, and Raspberry Pi 3 as the official FarmBot Genesis. Every tool, accessory, and software feature from the FarmBot ecosystem works seamlessly with P1.

### Streamlined Assembly 

– Inspired by v1.8’s simplified track design and integrated packaging, P1 gets you from unboxing to planting faster than ever.

### Open-Source & Upgradeable – 

Like FarmBot, P1 is built for makers and hackers. Extend your tracks, design custom tools, or reprogram your robot – nothing is proprietary, and nothing is off-limits.

### Proven Precision – 

Track-based movement delivers repeatable sub-millimeter accuracy, allowing you to manage any planting pattern without compacting your soil.

## Architecture at a Glance
P1 shares the same core architecture as FarmBot Genesis:

Tracks – V-slot aluminum extrusions that guide the gantry along the X-axis with precision and efficiency.

Gantry – Bridges the two tracks and moves via belt and pulley drive; also serves as the Y-axis linear guide.

Cross-Slide – Moves across the gantry along the Y-axis, carrying the Z-axis assembly.

Z-Axis – Provides vertical movement and mounts the Universal Tool Mount (UTM).

Universal Tool Mount – Magnetically attaches to all FarmBot-compatible tools, including the seeder, watering nozzle, soil sensor, weeder, and rotary tool.
