## WeNextFarm P1 Kit Contents

The WeNextFarm P1 package comes with everything you need to build and operate your own precision farming robot. The kit includes:

- Pre-cut and anodized aluminum extrusions (tracks, gantry main beam, columns, Z‑axis extrusion)

- Stainless steel lead screw (800 mm) with shaft coupler

-  30× pre‑assembled V‑wheels with sealed bearings

-  4× NEMA 17 stepper motors with rotary encoders

-  Farmduino microcontroller board and Raspberry Pi 3 (pre‑installed in the electronics box)

-  Waterproof IP67 power supply (24 V, 150 W) with power cord and cord protector

- 3× cable carriers (for X, Y, Z axes) with pre‑routed tubing and cable guides

- GT2 timing belts (X‑axis: 2 pcs, Y‑axis: 1 pc) and belt clips

-  Silicon water tubes (3 lengths) and full water system components (solenoid valve, pressure reducer, barb fittings, hose adapter)

-  Vacuum pump with EMI‑reduction PCB and air filter

-  Universal Tool Mount (UTM) with pogo‑pin PCB and magnetic coupler

-  Complete set of FarmBot‑compatible tools: seeder (with Luer‑lock needles), watering nozzle, soil sensor, weeder, and rotary tool with accessories

- Seed containers (troughs, trays, and bins) and tool bays

- Hardware kit: all M5 screws, nuts, washers, eccentric spacers, zip ties, nut bars of various lengths, belt sleeves, and assembly tools (hex drivers, wrenches)

 - LED lighting strip and all necessary cables (motor, encoder, UTM, camera, solenoid, vacuum pump)
 
- USB camera with mounting halves, Micro SD card with FarmBot OS pre‑loaded (and adapter)

- Quick start guide, calibration card, and warranty information

> Please note that the type and quantity of accessories may change from time to time due to material improvements and version iterations. We kindly request you refer to the actual items you receive for the most accurate configuration.
